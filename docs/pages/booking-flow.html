Key idea · the client never decides the price

The browser only shows the price — CATO's server recalculates it at the moment of charge. So even if someone edits the page to say "$1", they're still charged the real amount. Same for whether a car is available and whether the guest is verified: all decided on the server, never trusted from the app.

03

A real example — $65/day car, 3 days

A guest books a $65/day car for 3 days and adds Standard protection ($15/day). Here's the exact split (tax shown as a round ~10% for illustration — the real rate is set per state).

What the guest paysamount
Rental · $65 × 3 days$195.00
Standard protection · $15 × 3$45.00
Rental tax (~10%, illustrative)$19.50
Guest total$259.50

+ a refundable security deposit is held on the card, then released after the trip.

How that $259.50 splits

→ Host earnings (subtotal − 20% commission)$156.00
→ CATO commission (20% of $195)$39.00
→ CATO protection revenue$45.00
→ Tax remitted to government$19.50
− Stripe processing (~2.9% + 30¢)−$7.83
CATO gross profit (this booking)≈ $76.17

Protection revenue ($45) is gross — once a real insurance underwriter is connected, part of it pays the actual coverage cost, so net protection margin will be lower.

04

How CATO makes money — 4 streams

Every number below is a default you control from the admin panel — you can raise commission, price protection differently, or charge more for instant payouts, without touching code.

20%
Commission
Taken from every booking's rental subtotal. The core engine. Adjustable 0–40%, and can differ per car, superhost, or fleet deal.
$15–30/day
Protection plans
Standard & Premier are paid by the guest. Basic is free. Margin is yours until an insurer is wired in.
1.5%
Instant payout fee
Hosts who want their money now — instead of waiting the 24h hold — pay a small fee that goes to CATO.
+more
Add-ons & delivery
Extras (child seat, unlimited miles, delivery) flow through the same commission, so they lift the cut too.

Why the 24-hour payout hold matters

Hosts aren't paid the instant a trip ends — earnings are held 24 hours (72h for brand-new hosts under 3 trips). This window lets damage or disputes surface before the money leaves, protects against fraud, and is exactly what a guest's deposit and any incidental charges settle against.

The one gap to know about before real money moves

There's no insurance underwriter connected yet. The protection plans collect revenue, but the actual coverage promise behind them isn't backed by a real policy. Before taking live bookings at scale, that has to be real — it's the difference between selling protection and actually being able to pay a claim.
